4 · Application Discussion (10 min) (may vary; instructor’s call)

Read the prompt for your focus and jot a response; be ready to share.

  • Forestry/Forest Management: A forester maps timber stand types for a landowner. How does the choice of Symbology (colors, patterns) affect a non-GIS-savvy landowner’s understanding?
  • Wildlife Management: Mapping critical red-cockaded woodpecker habitat in a national forest, which essential map elements would you prioritize so managers can interpret it quickly?
  • Fisheries Management: Mapping fish sampling locations in a reservoir, how can map design distinguish species caught or water-quality values for a non-technical audience?
  • Parks Recreation & Tourism Management: How would a visitor map’s purpose (guidance) change your design choices compared with an internal maintenance map?
